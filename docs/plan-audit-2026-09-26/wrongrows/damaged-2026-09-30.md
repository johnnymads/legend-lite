# Damaged data, run 1 (2026-09-30): what the seeds could not see

`tools/wrongrows/damage.py` over every seed: per table a duplicate row (fresh primary key), a row with every nullable
column NULL, an orphan row per join (a key no other side has), and a row of type extremes. 11 data elements, 1,103
tables known from the schemas, 1,034 joins; 7,820 lines of `###Data`, never edited by hand (`receipts/…/damaged.pure`).

**Lite** loaded it after one product fix (below): 4,705 answers in 22 s; the damage changed the rows of about a thousand
tests (982 verdicts against the seed expectations, as expected). New loud failures: integer overflow on the extreme
values (6), one "scalar subquery returned more than one row" caused by the duplicate row (a shape the resolver assumes
unique: a defect for the list), the `hier::` services (2).

**The engine** ran 4,239 of 4,735: 446 tests errored in its loader because H2 counts a string's width in UTF-16 units
and the extreme row's non-BMP character took two (fixed in `damage.py`; run 2 is in the background), one batch killed.

**Engine against lite on the damaged data (run 1):** 4,096 EQUAL, 74 SPELLING (`NaN` vs `nan`, now normalised), 2 COUNT,
53 VALUES, 480 LITE-ONLY (the engine's loader errors), 14 ENGINE-ONLY.

**Of the 55 row disagreements, 37 are NEW (equal on the seeds), and all 37 are ONE class:** a `->count()` over an
association navigation from a parent with no children. The engine says 1, lite says 0. Pure's count of an empty
collection is 0; the engine counts the row its outer join supplies. **This is the same class as 9 of the 22 seed
disagreements** (`F0`–`F6`, `LE2`, `LE0`: parents with no children in the seeds). Attribution: legend-engine defect,
stage I (its SQL builder's `count` over an outer-joined navigation); lite is right; register row S22; not copied.

**What the damaged data did NOT find (yet):** a lite wrong answer that the seeds hid. Run 2 (the engine over the fixed
extremes) and the next damage kinds (milestone versions, ties on sort keys) are what W0.6's four resolver pushes wait
for. **The product fix the damage found:** `DuckDbAppenderLoad` dropped its staging table through the statement that had
just raised, so every load error read "Statement was closed" and the staging table leaked into every later test.
